Privacy Policy: Unified Messaging Hub

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Who uses this app

Unified Messaging Hub is a personal project used only by its developer, with the developer's own Google account. It is not available to the public.

What data it accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): messages, so they can be shown grouped by contact.
  • Google Contacts (read-only): names, email addresses, phone numbers and photos, to group messages by person.

Where data goes

  • Data is stored only on the owner's own computer or home server, which is not reachable from the public internet.
  • Data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising.
  • Data is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the owner deletes it. Messages deleted in Gmail are also deleted from the app. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
