Unified Messaging Hub (personal)

Unified Messaging Hub is a personal, self-hosted tool that shows its owner's own email and contacts in one place, grouped by person.

  • It is used by a single person: its developer. It is not offered to anyone else.
  • It runs only on the owner's own computer or home server. There is no hosted service and no sign-up.
  • It reads Gmail and Google Contacts with read-only access, and never shares that data with anyone.
